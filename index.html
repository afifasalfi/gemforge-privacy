Privacy Policy for Gembloom

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Gembloom ("the game") is a puzzle game published by Pearls Valley Academy ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Your game progress (level, stars, garden and settings) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob so it can be free to play. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your approximate location (from your IP address),
  • device and app information, and
  • how you interact with ads.

You can reset or turn off your advertising ID in your phone's Settings, under Google > Ads. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time from the game's settings.

Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data security and retention

The game does not send your progress to our servers, so there is nothing for us to store or delete. Uninstalling the game removes its local data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and publish the new version at this page.
